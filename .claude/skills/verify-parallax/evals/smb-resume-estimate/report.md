# A film from my NAS starts over instead of where I stopped

I play films straight off my NAS in Parallax. Normally when I close one partway through and open it again later, it picks up where I left off.

On some files it doesn't. I got most of the way through a film, closed the player near the end, and when I opened it again it started from the very beginning. The same thing happened on a second file. Other films on the same NAS remember where I stopped just fine.

The ones that forget seem to be films I had only just started copying onto the NAS, so the file hadn't finished transferring yet. The player did play them, but their position was never kept. I expected it to resume at the point where I closed it, the same as any other film.
